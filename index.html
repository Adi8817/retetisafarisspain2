Sample Itinerary

Kenya & Tanzania – 10 Days Step by Step

A detailed look at our most popular safari route. All itineraries are fully customisable to your dates and preferences.

Day 1 Arrival in Nairobi & Welcome

Arrival at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. Our driver-guide meets you and transfers you to your Nairobi hotel. Safari briefing, dinner and overnight in Nairobi.

Day 2 Nairobi → Lake Nakuru

Drive to Lake Nakuru National Park (4–5 hrs). Lunch, check-in and evening game drive. Highlights:

  • Flamingos and pelicans on the lake
  • White and black rhinos
  • Tree-climbing lions (if lucky)
Day 3 Lake Nakuru → Masai Mara Reserve

Morning game drive in Nakuru then transfer to Masai Mara (6 hrs). Afternoon game drive on arrival. Dinner and overnight at lodge or camp.

Days 4–5 Full-day Safari in Masai Mara

Two full days exploring the Mara. Optional hot air balloon safari at dawn. Picnic lunch on the Mara River banks.

  • The Big Five: lions, elephants, buffaloes, leopards, rhinos
  • Great Migration (Jul–Oct)
  • Optional Maasai village visit
Day 6 Mara → Serengeti, Tanzania

Cross the border at Isibania into Tanzania. Our Tanzanian team takes over and escorts you to Central Serengeti. Lunch en route; arrive at sunset.

Days 7–8 Serengeti Safari & Ngorongoro

Full-day Serengeti safari then transfer to Ngorongoro Crater. Descend into the crater (2,300 m) to observe incredibly concentrated wildlife: elephants, hippos, black rhinos, lions and spotted hyenas.

Days 9–10 Amboseli & Return to Nairobi

Transfer to Amboseli National Park for Kilimanjaro views. Afternoon and morning game drives. On Day 10, transfer to Nairobi for international flights.

  • Large elephant herds
  • Panoramic views of Kilimanjaro
  • Abundant birdlife
